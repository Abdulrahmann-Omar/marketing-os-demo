# Marketing OS demo

An interactive prototype of Marketing OS by BBIT × Fordeon: posters and reels for any brand, posted to every social
platform from one dashboard.

**Live demo:** https://abdulrahmann-omar.github.io/marketing-os-demo/

- Press **Guided tour** for the presenter walkthrough; **Reset demo** restores the starting state.
- English by default, with an Arabic switch.
- The whole demo is one self-contained file, `index.html`, which also opens straight from disk.

The data is simulated, and nothing in this demo posts to a real account. Three of the four brands are fictional; the
fourth is BBIT's own, with its look and wording taken from brightbrainsit.com.
